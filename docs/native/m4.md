# M4: Advanced shift supervision and trainer analysis

Task planning notes use the [dependency and blocker conventions](../milestones.md#task-dependencies-and-issue-creation). Dependencies name prerequisite artifacts; final paired evidence is a shared acceptance gate, not a reason to serialize client development.

3D extension (27 October 2026): existing tasks/handoffs remain unchanged. NAT-M4-10 through NAT-M4-12 and the additional exit criteria extend them under the [mandatory 3D contract](../three_d_simulation.md). Earlier optional detail/ECS or fallback language cannot waive the primary 3D world required from M2. New tasks remain planned until verified.

Status: planned. Depends on the accepted [M3 MVP](m3.md).

## Outcome

The native application supports complex training shifts, repeatable scenario variants, and analysis across attempts. Where a curated scene has backend-validated lane geometry, it can inspect a constrained local manoeuvre and its training-policy implications.

The primary product remains fleet-manager training. A local manoeuvre is a capability-gated simulation exercise; a route graph alone cannot justify clearance, visibility, or crossing a road marking.

## Dependencies

| Input | Required contract or artifact |
| --- | --- |
| Backend | Advanced triggers, scenario lineage, checkpoint/branch histories, trainer interventions, aggregate metrics |
| Curated manoeuvres | Lane geometry/visibility assumptions, vehicle envelope, capability declaration, allowed action taxonomy, revalidation rules |
| Product | Training rubric and accepted alternatives, exercise difficulty, trainer controls and assessment boundaries |
| Optional recorded inspection | Asset/provenance/calibration manifests and bounded backend-derived records; never an MVP dependency |

## Tasks

### NAT-M4-01: Advanced shift controls

**Dependencies:** [NAT-M3-06](m3.md#nat-m3-06-minimal-trainer-authoring), [BE-M4-01](../backend/m4.md#be-m4-01-extend-scenario-authoring-with-validated-dependencies), [BE-M4-02](../backend/m4.md#be-m4-02-improve-fleet-interaction-and-operational-consequences).

**Required inputs:** Role-gated intervention contracts and simulation/active-time definitions.

**Planned blockers / completion gates:** Trainer controls need backend permissions and audited effects; role revocation or overlapping events cannot produce unaudited local outcomes.

- Configure scenario variations with overlapping closures, stalled shuttles, charger outages, delayed dwell times, and reserve pressure.
- Display trigger preconditions and backend-selected activation times; trainer interventions carry actor identity and audit reasons.
- Provide pause/resume/speed controls only when the backend grants the role, with clear simulation-time and elapsed-time labels.
- Explain downstream impacts on route assignments, stops, charging windows, and coverage without recomputing outcomes in the client.

Output: advanced shift configuration, role-gated trainer controls, audited intervention UI, and backend-derived impact summaries.

Verification: overlapping closure/incident/charger fixtures expose the intended consequences; unauthorised or revoked controls cannot change the session, and every accepted intervention carries the correct actor, reason, and simulation-time reference.

### NAT-M4-02: Scenario lineage and repeatability

**Dependencies:** [NAT-M3-06](m3.md#nat-m3-06-minimal-trainer-authoring), [NAT-M3-05](m3.md#nat-m3-05-recorded-lead-up-and-immutable-review), [BE-M4-01](../backend/m4.md#be-m4-01-extend-scenario-authoring-with-validated-dependencies), [BE-M4-04](../backend/m4.md#be-m4-04-make-replay-and-branch-comparison-auditable).

**Required inputs:** Immutable manifests, seed/rubric compatibility and clone/branch contracts.

**Planned blockers / completion gates:** Retry/clone/branch must preserve parents and live sessions; incompatible attempts require exclusion or backend-defined comparison handling.

- Show immutable scenario version, seed, rule/rubric revisions, map revision, and source manifests before launch.
- Clone/edit templates without changing past attempts; distinguish retry, new scenario version, and branch from checkpoint.
- Launch backend-supported alternatives as separate runs with explicit lineage; never overwrite the active shift or its audit trail.
- Compare attempts only under compatible scenario/rubric versions and display exclusions or normalization supplied by the backend.

Output: scenario/run manifest view, clone/retry/branch launch flows, lineage navigation, and compatibility-aware attempt comparison.

Verification: the same manifest/seed selects the intended reproducible fixture; cloning and branching preserve parent records and the live shift, while incompatible rubric/scenario versions are excluded or explicitly handled according to backend comparison rules.

### NAT-M4-03: Trainer dashboard and patterns

**Dependencies:** [NAT-M3-07](m3.md#nat-m3-07-debrief-and-report-completion), [BE-M4-05](../backend/m4.md#be-m4-05-explain-assessment-patterns-across-attempts).

**Required inputs:** Role-scoped aggregate filters, denominators and export provenance.

**Planned blockers / completion gates:** Dashboard/drill-down/export counts must match backend fixtures, including empty groups and restricted records.

- Filter cohort/session results by scenario, incident type, period, response choice, rule exception, reserve breach, and coverage impact.
- Present distributions and counts with denominators, sample size, excluded cases, and scenario/rubric revisions.
- Drill from an aggregate pattern to concrete incident records and their evidence rather than treating a chart as its own explanation.
- Keep exports aligned with visible filters and include provenance; restrict trainee identity/details according to server permissions.

Output: filtered trainer dashboard, denominator-aware distributions, incident drill-down, and permission-scoped exports with provenance.

Verification: shared aggregate fixtures reproduce backend counts, exclusions, and sample sizes; drill-down and exports match the active filters, and empty groups or restricted identities cannot produce misleading totals or disclose unauthorised details.

### NAT-M4-04: Curated local-manoeuvre inspection

**Dependencies:** [NAT-M3-03](m3.md#nat-m3-03-concurrent-incident-workspace), [NAT-M3-10](m3.md#nat-m3-10-complete-surface-aware-3d-editing-and-placement), [BE-M4-03](../backend/m4.md#be-m4-03-gate-any-local-rule-exception-manoeuvre).

**Required inputs:** Lane/envelope/visibility overlays and capability revision.

**Planned blockers / completion gates:** Conditional: missing geometry or failed backend capability gate keeps local passing unavailable. Explicit unsupported state and network alternatives satisfy the core scope disposition.

- Discover supported manoeuvre capabilities from the backend; hide/disable geometry-dependent actions in ordinary graph-only scenes.
- Render supplied lane boundaries, permitted area, vehicle swept envelope, visibility limits, obstacle clearance, and candidate path.
- Label estimates and missing observations explicitly; planner confidence is not a safety probability or a permission to proceed.
- Require a fresh bounded proposal, named scenario-rule exception where applicable, reason, and final backend revalidation; an exception cannot bypass collision, visibility, unknown-clearance, or physical-fit constraints.

Output: capability-gated manoeuvre inspector with lane/envelope/visibility overlays and revision-bound exception review, or an explicit unsupported state when the shared geometry gate is not met.

Verification: graph-only, unknown-clearance, insufficient-visibility, changed-obstacle, and expired-proposal fixtures leave approval unavailable or rejected; a curated passing case is accepted only after backend revalidation, and a named exception cannot bypass hard constraints.

### NAT-M4-06: Evidence and comparison workspace

**Dependencies:** [NAT-M3-07](m3.md#nat-m3-07-debrief-and-report-completion), [NAT-M3-11](m3.md#nat-m3-11-add-immutable-3d-review-and-synthetic-onboard-view), [BE-M4-04](../backend/m4.md#be-m4-04-make-replay-and-branch-comparison-auditable), [BE-M4-05](../backend/m4.md#be-m4-05-explain-assessment-patterns-across-attempts).

**Required inputs:** Backend comparison metrics plus audited annotation contract and permissions.

**Planned blockers / completion gates:** Aligned evidence must preserve original scores/events; missing annotation API is an explicit backend contract handoff, not permission to store authoritative notes only in the client.

- Align immutable attempts by scenario time or incident anchor and label when events are not directly comparable.
- Compare decision delay, route overhead, missed stops, coverage, energy reserve, and accepted alternatives using backend metrics.
- Permit a trainer to annotate an attempt through audited backend records while preserving the original event/score evidence.
- Display reference solutions as scenario-specific accepted trade-offs, not a claim that the fastest choice is universally best.

Output: aligned attempt/reference workspace, backend-metric comparison, and audited trainer annotation controls that retain original evidence.

Verification: known anchor fixtures align correctly or explain non-comparability; annotations preserve original event/score records, alternative accepted outcomes retain their rubric meaning, and reference inspection cannot mutate the live attempt.

### NAT-M4-07: Optional recorded sensor inspection

**Dependencies:** [NAT-M3-05](m3.md#nat-m3-05-recorded-lead-up-and-immutable-review), [NAT-M3-11](m3.md#nat-m3-11-add-immutable-3d-review-and-synthetic-onboard-view).

**Required inputs:** Explicit include/defer decision and recorded source/calibration/model metadata.

**Planned blockers / completion gates:** Optional: absent scope approval or valid media keeps the inspector disabled. Missing/corrupt recordings cannot block core workflows or extend camera continuity past a branch.

- If explicitly included at scope lock, add a recorded camera/frame inspector with sequence, timestamp, geography, calibration, and source revision.
- Fetch bounded assets outside fleet snapshots; cancel stale downloads/decode jobs and limit CPU image memory/GPU texture allocations.
- Label backend-derived detections separately from recorded labels and synthetic ground truth; run no ML inference in the client.
- Disable recorded-camera continuation at the earliest world branch, including pre-trip edits; later incidents use simulation history and unavailable assets cannot block training workflows.

Output: a written include/defer scope decision and, if included, a bounded recorded-frame inspector with source/calibration/model metadata and explicit branch boundaries.

Verification: an included inspector distinguishes detections, recorded labels, and synthetic ground truth; missing/corrupt media or a world branch never creates false camera continuity or blocks core workflows. If deferred, the capability remains disabled and absent from required beta dependencies.

### NAT-M4-08: Work scheduling and fault isolation

**Required inputs:** Queue priorities, CPU/GPU budgets and cancellable asset/history work.

**Planned blockers / completion gates:** Saturated optional work must not starve durable events; stale completions, unbounded uploads or subsystem failures corrupting live state block completion.

- Add bounded priorities for user-visible state, durable incidents, geometry preparation, history pages, and optional media.
- Abort obsolete work using generation tokens and cooperative cancellation; cap in-flight CPU tasks and per-frame GPU upload time.

Verification: saturated optional media/detail work cannot starve durable incidents or exceed declared queue/resource limits; cancelled jobs cannot upload stale assets, and injected decode/audio/renderer failures retain a usable operational view.

## Additional 3D exit criteria

- Richer environment, spatial evidence and comparison extend the existing mandatory 3D world; they do not postpone its introduction.
- Authored observation origins and geometry revisions govern displayed visibility, with unknowns and optional manoeuvre limits intact.
- Semantic/spatial parity and mandatory P1 remain passing with the richer frozen content; exploratory P2 includes actual 3D load.

## Failure cases to exercise

- A trainer revokes an action capability or ends the exercise while a trainee has a proposal open.
- A graph-only scene receives a local-manoeuvre action; both the UI capability gate and backend rejection remain correct.
- A scenario changes its rubric between attempts; native and app show incompatibility rather than merge incomparable scores.
- Optional media stalls or exhausts its permitted allocation while a live incident arrives; incident controls stay responsive.
- Context recreation occurs after the detail tab is reparented/minimized; resources rebuild from retained immutable CPU data.
- A training branch is opened while a live shift continues; commands remain tied to the correct session and live state.

## Deliverables and exit evidence

- Advanced training/scenario controls and a usable native trainer analysis workspace with drill-down and filtered exports.
- One curated manoeuvre demonstration if the shared geometry/capability gate is met; otherwise explicit unsupported status with core features complete.
- Native/app parity for advanced scenario lineage, aggregate metrics, action eligibility, and report contents.
- Passing mandatory P1 results, resource and scheduling profiles, optional-subsystem failure evidence, and a ranked M5 optimization list.
- Written scope-lock record for optional recorded inspection; an omitted optional track cannot silently become a beta dependency.
