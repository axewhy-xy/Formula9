## BE-M3-01: Complete pre-trip planning and validation

**Dependencies:** [BE-M2-04](m2.md#be-m2-04-deliver-one-pre-trip-route-edit), [BE-M2-09](m2.md#be-m2-09-prove-the-first-cross-client-3d-exercise).

**Required inputs:** Timed closure semantics, avoid geometry, stop policy and complete curated constraint metadata.

**Planned blockers / completion gates:** Cannot approve atomic multi-shuttle plans without bounded arrival-time validation, stale-target rejection and a defined safe activation point; missing mandatory geometry remains UNKNOWN.

- Add disruptions with start/end times, edge closures, lane restrictions where metadata exists, and avoid polygons with validated geometry.
- Resolve route nodes to stable graph entities; validate directed traversal, turn restrictions, operating-area limits, declared vehicle dimensions, mandatory/temporary stops, and time-dependent closures.
- Use a bounded iterative arrival-time check so route duration and closure windows agree; report uncertainty or infeasibility explicitly when a stable validated result cannot be found.
- Generate a bounded set of distinct detours with explanations of affected constraints, service changes, delay, distance, and energy cost.
- Support draft revisions, comparison, and atomic approval for an explicit shuttle set. Define when an approved route becomes active, such as before departure or at a safe transition point.
- Cancel superseded preview jobs, reject stale results, and avoid installing partial plans when one target has changed.

Output: complete pre-trip service and a fixture suite for closures, turn restrictions, fit, stops, and operating area.

Verification: boundary timestamps, self-intersecting polygons, unreachable required stops, insufficient geometry, wrong-way travel, and approval/closure races are covered.

## BE-M3-02: Complete in-trip decision management

**Dependencies:** [BE-M2-05](m2.md#be-m2-05-deliver-one-incident-and-one-battery-decision), [BE-M2-03](m2.md#be-m2-03-persist-commands-and-recoverable-state), [BE-M3-01](m3.md#be-m3-01-complete-pre-trip-planning-and-validation).

**Required inputs:** Concurrent incident scripts, branch lineage and versioned active-decision-time policy.

**Planned blockers / completion gates:** Complete only when wait/escalation/manual detours revalidate at application, previews leave live state untouched and competing incident/resource changes invalidate stale options.

- Schedule multiple incidents by scenario time or deterministic condition; preserve affected shuttle state while the remaining fleet continues.
- Return option sets with rule checks, route revision, scene constraints, predicted consequences, evidence availability, and expiry.
- Implement wait with a duration/reevaluation trigger, escalation with persistent stopped state, approved road-level detour, and manually drafted route validation.
- Separate preview branches from the live session with parent checkpoint, command history, seed, source, and branch identifiers.
- Revalidate every approval at application time; a previously valid option may become unavailable after another decision changes fleet resources.
- Measure active trainee decision time separately from simulation time and record pause/disconnect intervals under a versioned assessment policy.

Output: incident workflow APIs, branch/replay APIs, decision timing policy, and concurrent-incident fixtures.

Verification: stale-option approval, incident resolved by another client, wait expiration, escalation without automatic release, and manual-route validation failures yield explicit outcomes.

## BE-M3-03: Implement battery and charging operations

**Dependencies:** [BE-M2-05](m2.md#be-m2-05-deliver-one-incident-and-one-battery-decision), [BE-M2-09](m2.md#be-m2-09-prove-the-first-cross-client-3d-exercise), [BE-M3-01](m3.md#be-m3-01-complete-pre-trip-planning-and-validation).

**Required inputs:** Capacity/rate/efficiency units, uncertainty margins, availability windows and minimum coverage rules.

**Planned blockers / completion gates:** Atomic vehicle/charger/replacement plans require reachable depot paths and deterministic arrival/release transitions; double booking or partial swaps block completion.

- Model energy in documented units, including travel, dwell/auxiliary use, reserve, charger rate/efficiency, capacity, and availability windows.
- Produce per-shuttle projected energy and configurable uncertainty margins across the assigned plan.
- Implement charger occupancy, maintenance, reservations, arrival, charge completion, release, and cancellation as explicit state transitions.
- Evaluate return-to-depot, charge without replacement, and planned shuttle swap options against minimum service coverage and reserve constraints.
- Commit multi-resource plans atomically: vehicle assignment, charger reservation, replacement shuttle, effective time, and route version are one decision.
- Show infeasible plans with the binding reason; scenario settings distinguish hard constraints from permitted training tradeoffs.

Output: charging schedule/preview/approval services, fleet coverage summaries, and resource-conflict fixtures.

Verification: charger double-booking, swap cancellation, delayed arrival, reserve breach prediction, charger outage, and reduced service capacity produce consistent outcomes in both clients.

## BE-M3-04: Add minimum trainer authoring and assignment

**Dependencies:** [BE-M2-01](m2.md#be-m2-01-define-the-training-domain-and-versioned-contracts), [BE-M2-03](m2.md#be-m2-03-persist-commands-and-recoverable-state).

**Required inputs:** Scenario/reference/rubric definitions and trainer/trainee authorization policy; BE-M3-01/02/03 field contracts.

**Planned blockers / completion gates:** Publication must validate references and freeze the attempt revision while withholding reference answers. Shared M2 operator credentials alone do not satisfy trainer/trainee authorization.

- Persist immutable published scenario revisions with a mutable draft, initial fleet, routes, disruptions, incidents, batteries, chargers, and difficulty parameters.
- Validate all referenced scene entities, trigger dependencies, durations, and option rules before publication.
- Store multiple acceptable reference decisions and criterion-level rubrics; do not require a single scripted answer.
- Add trainer/trainee roles and session access checks at the backend boundary; hiding a UI control is insufficient authorization.
- Assign a scenario revision to a session and freeze its rubric/parameters for that attempt; later edits create a new revision.
- Keep unpublished/reference-only content out of trainee responses until the configured debrief stage.

Output: authoring, publication, assignment, role enforcement, and reference solution contracts.

Verification: incomplete drafts cannot publish; unauthorized trainer actions fail; scenario edits cannot change an in-progress assessment; reference answers do not leak through ordinary session snapshots.

## BE-M3-05: Persist debrief, score explanations, and exports

**Dependencies:** [BE-M3-02](m3.md#be-m3-02-complete-in-trip-decision-management), [BE-M3-03](m3.md#be-m3-03-implement-battery-and-charging-operations), [BE-M3-04](m3.md#be-m3-04-add-minimum-trainer-authoring-and-assignment).

**Required inputs:** Versioned rubric, unavailable-metric rules, safety caps and report retention policy.

**Planned blockers / completion gates:** Needs committed outcome/timing evidence and bounded export jobs. Duplicate attempts, leaked references or exports that disagree with retained results block acceptance.

- Derive incident records and assessment results from committed evidence: rule outcomes, active decision time, attempts, service impact, route efficiency, and energy/resource consequences.
- Version scoring formulas and weights, define unavailable metrics, and keep predicted consequences distinct from simulated observed outcomes.
- Fail the attempt for a critical unsafe outcome regardless of speed; apply explicit versioned penalties/caps to other violations so timing points cannot erase safety failures.
- Return criterion-level calculation evidence and the accepted alternatives after completion; retain original result versions if a new rubric rescoring is requested.
- Export structured JSON and spreadsheet-friendly CSV with units, manifests, provenance, decisions, rejected attempts, and final score explanations; add an HTML report containing score components, reasons, route changes, energy/service effects, and reference explanations.
- Run report generation as a bounded asynchronous job with status, retry, cancellation, and output retention; neutralize spreadsheet formula prefixes in user text.

Output: persisted assessment record, debrief API, JSON/CSV/HTML artifacts, and reference-result fixtures.

Verification: duplicate command retries do not add attempts; equivalent acceptable decisions score as specified; missing data never silently becomes a perfect score; exports match committed records.

Output: complete-shift benchmark report, contract conformance results, bounded-queue tests, and cross-client integration evidence.

## BE-M3-07: Validate surface-aware routes and volume clearance

**Dependencies:** [BE-M2-07](m2.md#be-m2-07-establish-the-shared-3d-scene-and-spatial-contract), [BE-M2-08](m2.md#be-m2-08-run-authoritative-road-constrained-3d-motion), [BE-M3-01](m3.md#be-m3-01-complete-pre-trip-planning-and-validation).

**Required inputs:** Authored decks, headroom, support transitions, body envelopes and depot approach geometry.

**Planned blockers / completion gates:** Missing support/fit evidence cannot pass. Requires stacked-road, safe-splice and swept-clearance cases that preserve exact surface identity.

- Extend route/placement validation with stable surface/deck IDs, vehicle height/width/envelope, overhang clearance, support continuity and explicit unknown geometry.
- Keep stacked roads distinct despite overlapping XY coordinates; topology, authored transition and support geometry must all permit a change of level.
- Validate route execution/safe splice and depot/bay paths against 3D proxies; add overlap, low-headroom, invalid-support and swept-movement fixtures.

Output: surface-aware validation results, 3D clearance/placement checks, structured spatial failure locations and vertical-constraint fixtures.

Verification: a bridge vehicle does not falsely collide with traffic underneath, insufficient headroom is rejected, and neither a planar route nor a client pick can bypass missing mandatory surface/fit evidence.

## BE-M3-08: Add revisioned 3D scenario placement and depot semantics

**Dependencies:** [BE-M3-04](m3.md#be-m3-04-add-minimum-trainer-authoring-and-assignment), [BE-M3-07](m3.md#be-m3-07-validate-surface-aware-routes-and-volume-clearance), [BE-M3-03](m3.md#be-m3-03-implement-battery-and-charging-operations).

**Required inputs:** Dimensioned obstacle placements, scene revision rules and exclusive bay definitions.

**Planned blockers / completion gates:** Placement/publication and bay occupancy must share authoritative spatial entities; stale edits, unsupported placements and overlapping exclusive bays must reject atomically.

- Accept trainer-authored obstacle position/orientation/dimensions and explicit surface binding through validated scenario drafts; restrict live changes to audited authorised interventions.
- Pin static/proxy changes to immutable scene/spatial revisions and dynamic obstacle changes to committed object revisions; invalidate affected candidates atomically.
- Bind charger/depot bays, approach paths and vehicle occupancy to the same spatial entities used in schedule execution, without granting charging from a visual animation.

Output: 3D placement/publication contracts, intervention audit records, dimension/surface constraints and depot/bay occupancy fixtures.

Verification: invalid dimensions, out-of-area/unsupported placement, stale edits and overlapping exclusive bays fail predictably; published edits cannot rewrite active attempts, and clients receive one consistent placement/charging outcome.

## Additional 3D exit criteria

- Multi-level, headroom, support and placement cases pass in the authoritative validator and agree with both clients.
- The complete authored route/incident/charging/debrief exercise and synthetic-view/replay paths use one pinned 3D scene lineage.
- All mandatory P1 gates pass for the full 3D workload; original MVP evidence alone cannot close the new tasks.

## M3 exit gate

1. A trainer publishes and assigns a small exercise; a trainee completes pre-trip, incidents, and charging, then receives a reproducible debrief and export.
2. Both clients independently complete the same workflow and can observe the same session without conflicting clocks or divergent results.
3. Route, incident, charger, permission, and revision-conflict cases have explicit rejected outcomes; failure cannot create a partial approved plan.
4. P1 budgets are measured during the complete workflow, including background export and preview load; unresolved misses block added complexity.

Deferred to M4: richer scenario authoring, calibrated difficulty, aggregate debrief patterns, expanded replay comparisons, and any constrained local passing manoeuvre.
